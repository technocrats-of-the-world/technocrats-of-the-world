# Technocrats of the World - Project Charter

## Project Overview

**Project Name**: Technocrats of the World  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: September 28, 2025  
**Status**: Initiated  

**Mission**: To create an open-source, blockchain-powered platform that democratizes urban and community problem-solving through transparent, citizen-driven processes that eliminate corruption and maximize community impact.

## Project Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. **Universal Community Problem-Solving**: Enable citizens to directly identify, prioritize, and fund local or global improvements across all major domains (infrastructure, education, health, environment, etc.)
2. **Global & Local Scalability**: Create a reusable platform that communities of any size can deploy, adapt, and scale
3. **Transparency & Accountability**: Implement blockchain and open processes to make misappropriation and exclusion impossible
4. **Quality Assurance**: Establish audited, multi-level verification for high-quality, measurable impacts

### Secondary Objectives
1. **Citizen Empowerment**: Facilitate direct, democratic participation in solving pressing challenges
2. **Collective Intelligence**: Harness global expertise, peer review, and local knowledge for innovation
3. **Knowledge Sharing**: Build a global network of best-practice, open knowledge, and reusable solutions
4. **Sustainable Social Impact**: Advance UN SDGs and align with community priorities across domains

## Success Criteria

### Quantitative Metrics
- **Platform Adoption**: 10+ cities/regions/projects live in 2 years
- **Project Volume**: ₹100 crore+ in citizen-directed projects in 3 years
- **Transparency**: 100% of funds/processes auditable on blockchain
- **Quality**: 90%+ citizen/end-user satisfaction ratings

### Qualitative Metrics
- **Democratic Participation**: Sustained, diverse civic engagement
- **Replication**: Local solutions reused by other communities
- **Open Ecosystem**: Active global developer and practitioner community

## Stakeholder Analysis

### Primary Stakeholders
- **Citizens**: End users, problem-identifiers, solution evaluators
- **Solution Providers/Contractors**: Execute projects/solutions of all types
- **NGOs/Legal Entities**: Implementing organizations
- **Auditors/Validators**: Quality and impact assurance agents

### Secondary Stakeholders
- **Governments**
- **Funders/Donors**
- **Technology/Academic Partners**

## Scope Definition

### In Scope
- Full-stack platform and architecture
- Modular, multi-domain workflows
- Blockchain integration for transparency
- Citizen voting and participatory budgeting
- Pilot implementation (e.g., Bengaluru)
- Open-source community documentation

### Out of Scope
- Direct control of government resources/mandates
- Operations outside of civic/community/public problem spaces
